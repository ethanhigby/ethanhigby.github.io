About

Ethan Higby is a student at the Newhouse School of Communications and the Maxwell School of Public Affairs in Syracuse University. He is currently a junior and is slated to graduate in May of 2029.

Ethan has interned for a local radio station in Upstate New York called "The Moose" where he recorded and edited advertisements and served as a DJ for the station, and would also record Weather Forecasts for the station. He also has experience in writing stories for Syracuse University classes weather it be general news, reaction stories, and event coverage.

When not in classes, Ethan participates in multiple shows for the Orange Television Network, including "Think Tank" and "Peaked".

Ethan's career goals include working in Political Journalism for a news outlet through the medium of social media.
